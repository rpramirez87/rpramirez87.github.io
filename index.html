Sun Communities & Sun Outdoors · Senior iOS Developer (iOS Lead)

NavigateAI: AI-Automated Property Inspections

Inspections cut from 15–20 min to 5–7 min across 500 properties
Problem
On-site staff spent 15–20 minutes per inspection taking and manually uploading photos, then filling report fields by hand.
My role
Led the integration with a third-party AI vendor and defined the iOS architecture end to end.
Approach
Staff now record a single video walkthrough. The vendor's AI performs photo-based damage detection and auto-fills report fields, and results flow back to the app through a webhook-based data sync I designed.
Outcome
Average inspection time dropped to 5–7 minutes, rolled out across 500 properties.

Offline-First Inspection Reports

Lost reports reduced from 5% to zero
Problem
Many properties have poor cellular coverage, and about 5% of inspection reports were being lost when connectivity dropped mid-submission.
Approach
Engineered local offline support so staff can complete and save full reports on device, with data syncing once a connection is available.
Outcome
Lost reports went from 5% to zero, and staff no longer have to redo inspections.

CVS Pharmacy: SwiftUI Migration

40% of the app (30+ screens) migrated, 90 seconds off launch time
Context
The CVS Pharmacy app serves millions of customers across mobile payments, ExtraCare offers, prescriptions, and Curbside Pickup.
Approach
Drove the migration of 30+ screens from UIKit to SwiftUI, and partnered with the architecture team on a VIPER to Clean Architecture migration to improve testability. Championed accessibility, profiled performance with Instruments, and mentored junior engineers.
Outcome
40% of the app converted to SwiftUI, with launch time cut by 90 seconds.
